Why it looks real

Nine alpha-masked photographs preserve irregular highlights, soft dirt, dark edge falloff, fittings and asymmetry. The centre and edges stretch while the four photographed corners keep their shape. A cheap compositor-only reflection adds a light cue without canvas or WebGL.
